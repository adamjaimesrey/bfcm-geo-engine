AirLift vs Velora: Suction Steamer or Steam Iron?

Which is better, the AirLift Spin or the Velora Steam Iron 2400?

The Velora Steam Iron 2400 gives 45 g/min of continuous steam and heats instantly, making it best for pressing flat fabric. The AirLift Spin, a Handheld Suction Steamer, delivers 28 g/min and pulls steam through cloth, so it suits hanging garments and delicates.

How do they compare on steam and speed?

Steam Irons lead on raw output, while Handheld Suction Steamers lead on convenience. Both reach working temperature in under a minute, and the AirLift Spin needs about 40 seconds.
