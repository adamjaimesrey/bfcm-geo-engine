What is the best handheld steamer for travel?

The PocketPuff Mini Steamer is our top pick for travel: at 1000 W it heats in 25 seconds and fits in a carry-on. The Brisa Handheld Steamer trades size for more steam at 22 g/min.

Which Handheld Steamer packs smallest?

The PocketPuff folds to about 18 cm, which is 4 cm shorter than the Brisa.
